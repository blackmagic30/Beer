Privacy and deletion

Account deletion starts as a review request.

If you can sign in, open Account, choose Support, then use "Request deletion review". If you cannot sign in, choose "Account deletion request" below and include the account email so ownership can be checked.

Support contact

Use this form until launch contacts are final.

A dedicated support email, privacy contact, legal entity, service address if required, and expected response window should be published before launch. Do not include passwords, card numbers, private keys, or ID documents in support messages.

Security

Report sensitive issues privately.

Choose "Security report" for suspected account, privacy, payment, storage, or authorization issues. Please do not exploit bugs or access accounts/venues you do not own.
