Why bars should care

  • Keep happy hours, tap-list highlights and beer prices accurate where locals are already searching.
  • Show verified data instead of stale listings or user guesses.
  • Receive wrong-price reports and update requests in one review queue.
  • See aggregated demand signals like venue clicks, price reveals and searched beers nearby.

Privacy-safe insights

Venue insights are aggregate only. We do not sell individual user clickstream, exact user location or account details to venues. Counts are directional while the beta grows.

Responsible use: prices may change, service is subject to RSA obligations, and availability is never guaranteed.
