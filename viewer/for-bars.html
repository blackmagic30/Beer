FAQ

What bar owners usually ask first

Is it free to update my beer list?
Yes. Basic is free and is designed for profile, tap-list, price, happy-hour and special updates.
Can other bars see my data?
No. Plus and Pro analytics are aggregate and privacy-safe. Competitor-level private data is not exposed.
How do monthly reports work?
Reports summarise your own venue activity and suburb search trends once there is enough data.
How do I claim my bar?
Register interest below. Admin will review the request and assign your account to the venue if approved.
What is the difference between Basic, Plus, and Pro?
Basic keeps your listing current for free. Plus adds suburb analytics and monthly reports. Pro adds premium display metadata such as a highlighted name and featured-special eligibility.
