Data requests

Signed-in users can download a quick account export and schedule account deletion from Account. The workflow has a seven-day cancellation window and authorised-operator execution. After deletion commits, Pint Path automatically queues a completion notice, tracks delivery without exposing its destination in admin, and purges the recipient ciphertext on verified delivery, audited terminal resolution, or the retention deadline.
