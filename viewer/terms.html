Beta terms

Melbourne-only field-test terms.

This private beta helps test whether verified local price data is useful. Keep submissions accurate, current, and responsible.

Use responsibly

Melbourne Beer Map is for adults 18+ in Victoria. Prices, happy hours, and availability may change. Check with the venue before ordering, and venues may refuse service under RSA obligations.

Location

Near-me features use one-time browser location only after you tap the location control. Distances are approximate straight-line estimates, not walking or driving directions.

Community data

Data is community-submitted and verified where possible. Submissions stay pending until reviewed or sufficiently verified. False or abusive submissions can be rejected, fraud-flagged, or lose contribution eligibility.

Billing and rewards

Demo billing may be enabled during field testing and does not take live payment. Do not use live payments unless Stripe test mode has passed. Partner venue credits and free-beer redemption are not active.
