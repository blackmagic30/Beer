Trust centre

How Pint Path keeps beer-price data useful.

Pint Path is built around verified Melbourne venue data, contributor accountability, private evidence, and privacy-safe venue insights.

Our rule of thumb

Public users see approved venue and price data. Raw photos, reviewer notes, account details, and individual analytics stay private.

Verified data

Review before publishing

Submissions can stay pending until evidence is checked. Approved records may be normalized before they appear on the public map.

Private evidence

Photos are not public

Menu photos, receipts, tap-list images, upload-location proof, OCR evidence, and reviewer notes are treated as private review material.

Venue insights

Aggregate only

Paid venue reports are designed around suburb and venue-level trends with privacy thresholds, not individual user clickstreams.

What happens to data

From upload to public map

This is the path we are building toward for every contributor upload.

  1. Submit.A signed-in contributor uploads a venue, beer price, happy-hour update, or source photo.
  2. Protect.Raw evidence stays private and is linked to the contributor account for review and anti-abuse checks.
  3. Review.Reviewers verify freshness, serving size, venue, price, and whether points should be awarded.
  4. Publish.Only approved, normalized venue and beer-price data is shown publicly on the map.
