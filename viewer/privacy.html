Privacy summary

Simple, honest beta data use.

We collect only what helps the field test work: accounts, submissions, searches, clicks, feedback, and aggregate usage events.

What we collect

Account email, 18+ confirmation timestamp, saved items, venue submissions, reports, requests, feedback, and app usage events such as searches, filters, venue views, and price reveals.

How it is used

We use aggregated analytics to improve venue coverage, understand demand, and decide which Melbourne venues need verification next. We do not track continuous GPS movement or store exact user movement trails.

Location

Location is only used when you choose “Use my location”. We use it in your browser to show nearby venues, approximate distances, and happy hours near you. We do not continuously track your location, store precise coordinates by default, or sell individual location data to venues.

What is not sold

Individual user clickstream is not sold or exposed to venues. Admin dashboards show aggregate counts only. You can request deletion or corrections through the feedback form during the beta.
