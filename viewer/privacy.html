Privacy summary

Simple, honest beta data use.

We collect only what helps the field test work: accounts, submissions, searches, clicks, feedback, and aggregate usage events.

What we collect

Account email, 18+ confirmation timestamp, saved items, venue submissions, reports, requests, feedback, and app usage events such as searches, filters, venue views, map pin clicks, happy-hour interest, beer-list views, and price reveals.

How it is used

We use aggregated analytics to improve venue coverage, understand demand, decide which Melbourne venues need verification next, and produce privacy-safe suburb reports for paid venue tiers. Venue reports use thresholds so tiny buckets are suppressed.

Location

Location is only used when you choose “Use my location”. Browsing location stays in your browser for nearby venues, approximate distances, and happy hours. Search and click analytics may include coarse context such as approximate suburb, selected radius, and distance bucket, but not exact latitude/longitude. If you submit venue data, you can also save a one-time private upload-location proof so reviewers know the update was captured near the venue before awarding points. That proof can stay on your device for up to 12 hours or until the submission succeeds. We do not continuously track your location, publish precise coordinates, or sell individual location data to venues.

What is not sold

Individual user clickstream, names, account emails, exact user locations, and private source evidence are not sold or exposed to venues. Admin and venue dashboards show aggregate counts only. You can request deletion or corrections through the feedback form during the beta.
